# V2.2.20 — Layer Egg Production-to-Sales Ledger

Pooled Layer egg revenue is now attributed from the accumulated **unsold egg pool**, rather than only from production recorded on the sale date.

## Business rules

- Daily Layer Records add egg production to the owning cycle's pool.
- Canonical pooled-sale quantity is crates; `crates_count` is used first and `egg_production / 30` is the fallback.
- Direct-cycle egg sales remove quantity from that cycle's pool.
- Shared Layer egg sales allocate quantity and revenue according to each cycle's share of unsold egg stock available on the sale date.
- A rearing/non-producing Layer cycle has no unsold egg stock and receives 0% of egg revenue.
- Partial sales leave the remaining cycle ownership in the pool for later sales.
- If recorded unsold egg stock is below the quantity sold, the platform does not invent stock or a split; the sale remains unallocated until source data is corrected.
- The source sale remains one Sales Record. Allocation rows are reporting ownership only and cannot double-count farm/Layer revenue.
- Editing/deleting Layer Daily Records or Layer egg sales replays later pooled allocations chronologically.

## UX language

`Shared between cycles` is used when a known production type has multiple contributing cycles. General sales display `Not applicable` for cycle rather than looking incomplete. Cycle Profitability explicitly reports shared revenue that was included and only warns when some shared revenue genuinely cannot yet be assigned.
